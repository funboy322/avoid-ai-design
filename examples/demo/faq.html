Getting started

How long does it take to get my first chart?

About five minutes. Drop our snippet into your app or point Cadence at your warehouse, and events start streaming in live. Your first dashboard is one visual query away, no SQL required.

Do I need to know SQL?

No. Dashboards, funnels, and retention are all built from a visual query builder. SQL is there the moment you want it, but it is never a requirement to get an answer.

Can I import historical data?

Yes. Backfill from your warehouse or a CSV, and Cadence reconciles it with live events automatically, so a chart drawn today still reflects last year.

Billing and plans

What counts as an event?

Any tracked action: a page view, a click, a server-side call. You only pay for events you keep; anything you sample out or filter before storage does not count against your plan.

What happens if I go over my plan's events?

Nothing breaks. We flag it, keep collecting, and prorate the overage at your tier's rate. No sampling you did not ask for, no surprise shutoffs mid-month.

Can I change plans later?

Any time, up or down. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle and we prorate the difference. Compare the tiers on the pricing page.

Data and privacy

Where is my data stored?

In-region by default, US or EU, on SOC 2 Type II infrastructure. You pick the region at setup, and it never leaves it without your say-so.

Can I delete a user's data?

Yes. A single call removes a user and every event tied to them, across both live and historical stores, within 24 hours, and returns a receipt you can hand to an auditor.

Security and access

Is Cadence SOC 2 compliant?

Yes, SOC 2 Type II. You can pull the current report straight from your dashboard, or request it from our security team before you sign anything.

Do you support SSO and roles?

SAML SSO and role-based access ship on the Scale plan. Starter and Pro include team workspaces with basic roles, so you can invite the whole team from day one.
